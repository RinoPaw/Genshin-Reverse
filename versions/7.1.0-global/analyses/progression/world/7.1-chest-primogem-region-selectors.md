## Localization gap: solved for all Mondstadt/Liyue

The old report said none of the 578 `WorldAreaConfigData.areaNameTextMapHash`
values matched the server's `TextMapCHS.json`. The cause is two
**disjoint localization hash namespaces** in the original resource
repository, plain `TextMapCHS.json` and `TextMap_MediumCHS.json`.
The server resource snapshot ships plain only.

The 7.1 `DimbreathBot/AnimeGameData@792978e5503ecfba73dcb3562ed44a0d35a2abe2`
TextMap was verified independently against
`MeChen618/AstaPS-Resource@85b81a691fcf3d9e8e7db50a8febc8f9043bc084`:
all **578** `(sceneID,areaID1,areaID2)` keys and all matching name-hash
values agree. The decoded CHS mapping resolves **550 via Medium**,
**0 via plain**, and explicitly leaves **28 without a text entry**.
See [all 578 pinned area names](7.1-world-area-names-chs.json)
and [reproducer](../../../../../scripts/resolve-7.1-world-area-textmap.py).
The hosted verification is
[Actions run 38500599254](https://github.com/RinoPaw/Genshin-Reverse/actions/runs/38500599254).

**100/100** Mondstadt and Liyue area definitions are now named:
35 in cityId=1 (6 level-1 + 29 level-2), 65 in cityId=2
(10 level-1 + 55 level-2). The 28 missing translations do not affect
either of those city IDs. All seven Inazuma level-1 and 45 level-2 names
are also recovered.

## Candidate `rewards.toml` selector policy (not an implemented syntax)

Default high-tier entries remain `kind=common/exquisite/precious/luxurious`.
For *genuinely ordinary* treasure chests in old Mondstadt/Liyue, a
`kind + cityId` low-tier override applies except for exact
`scene + area1/area2` high-tier exceptions. Newer region IDs must be
excluded from the old low-tier rule until their native rewards are
confirmed. Priority is more specific selectors first, without merging
reward table contents.

`scene:area1:area2` and `cityId` select a geographic rule; they
do not identify chest quality. Keep source classification, special
puzzles, Shrines/Remarkable, random drops, and successful claim
acknowledgement separate. Do not double pay Primogems or Adventure EXP
from `ChestDrop.json` and a fixed reward config.

## Remaining precise limits

- 28 unrelated hashes have no CHS localization record in the pinned
  two-table 7.1 snapshot; their area IDs are preserved in the mapping.
- Remarkable Tsurumi zero-Primogem exceptions require correct prefab
  classification, not a blanket regional zero reward.
- Source `drop_tag` grade (`低级`, `中级`, etc.) cannot establish
  low-vs-high regional Primogem status.
- Runtime chest payout behavior has not been changed. This document
  establishes the geography and the names, and identifies what still
  requires native per-chest proof.
