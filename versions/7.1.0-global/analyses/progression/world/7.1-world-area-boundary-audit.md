# 7.1 WorldArea: original geometry and native selection

**Current status (2026-10-14): original 7.1 binary completely decoded; native selection confirmed; runtime packets not captured.**

The exact 7.1 client checks **parent X/Z polygons before child X/Z
polygons**, with an inclusive **child Y range** and ordered first-match
priority. All **13** original WorldArea binary payloads from official
Sophon bundle `0/24230448` are SHA-256 verified and completely decoded.

The native geometry has **90 parent records, 480 child polygon records,
474 distinct child IDs and 9,177 vertices**. Its area-ID inventory matches
the historical 7.0-lineage community dump: the same **14 configured child
IDs are absent from WorldArea geometry in the exact original**.

Only **Scene 3** differs geometrically. A parent-ID + child-ID based
comparison identifies eight changed child IDs:
`5502, 5602, 5608, 5701, 5702, 5703, 5802, 5804`.
Compared with the historical dump, `5602` adds **5 vertices**,
`5701` adds **3** and `5702` adds **2**, accounting for the native
total's ten extra vertices. `5705` changes only **list order**:
it swaps position with `5701` beneath parent `57`, with no
independent geometry change.

Reproducible evidence: [complete native binary decode](7.1-native-world-area-decoded-summary.json),
[per-ID geometry delta](7.1-native-world-area-geometry-delta.json),
[byte-verified comparison workflow](https://github.com/RinoPaw/Genshin-Reverse/actions/runs/38494967352).
The old boundary-crossing witnesses have now been rescanned against
**actual native 7.1** polygons: **22** positive crossing IDs persist
(21 in Scene 3, one in Scene 7). Area IDs **5502 and 5602** are no longer
crossing cases. An independent valid-polygon topology check proves
both native children are **entirely covered by their declared parents**
in X/Z (outside area exactly zero). See
[native crossing analysis](7.1-native-world-area-crossings-summary.json)
and [independent containment proof](7.1-native-world-area-target-topology.json).
What remains is verifying higher-level runtime event/packet timing.
No original binary assets are committed.

## Four crossing parent-child polygons

For each child polygon, calculate the arithmetic mean of the X/Z vertices.
Check that witness against the child polygon and its declared parent. The
following witnesses lie **inside the child but outside the parent**:

| Declared parent | Child | Witness X/Z | Child vertices inside parent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 31 | 3205 | (976.4, 6569.7) | 9 of 17 |
| 33 | 3302 | (2731.1, 4476.4) | 4 of 12 |
| 41 | 4104 | (1506.5, 2698.5) | 0 of 25 |
| 52 | 5210 | (-544.5, 15059.2) | 2 of 9 |

These IDs occur with the same declared parents and **identical polygon vertices**
in the historical 7.0 geometry source. The parent relationships in `WorldAreaConfigData.json`
and the child records themselves agree; **the geographical outlines
disagree**. This is a genuine boundary-consistency warning, not a discovered
replacement parent ID. A polygon centroid can lie outside a concave polygon,
so the audit explicitly requires the witness also to be inside the child.

The server currently handles `EnterWorldAreaReq` by accepting an
`areaId` *and* an `areaType` from the client. This shows that the
wire request distinguishes the area type, but does **not** establish the
client's precedence when overlapping LEVEL_1/LEVEL_2 polygons match.
The client may test levels independently, impose parent constraints, or use
other precedence conditions. We have **not** recovered the method body that
decides this for the exact 7.1 binary.

The public 7.1 native metadata indices in `RinoPaw/Genshin-Reverse`
contain obfuscated type/method identities and cannot alone prove the
WorldArea polygon selector's runtime semantics. A verified decompilation
trace from the pinned executable, or a controlled observation of
`EnterWorldAreaReq` at the witness positions, is required to promote a
specific precedence rule to **CONFIRMED**.

## Broader interior scan: 20 children with positive sample witnesses

The original centroid check missed boundary slivers and children whose
vertex-average lies inside their parent. The collector now also samples
the center of every cell in a **17 × 17 grid over each child polygon's
bounding box**, retaining only points strictly inside the child polygon.
For **20 distinct child area IDs**, at least one such interior point lies
outside the child record's declared parent polygon:

`201, 2102, 2504, 3003, 3004, 3204, 3205, 3302, 3407, 3408,
3604, 4004, 4005, 4103, 4104, 4109, 4702, 5209, 5210, 5602`.

These include the four centroid conflicts already listed. An additional
**16 crossings** would be missed by a centroid-only test. The following
selected records demonstrate why a blanket parent-gating policy needs
native validation:

| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| 4104 | 41 | 0 | 195 | (1138.52, 2715.03) | 47 |

| 3205 | 31 | 128 | 90 | (748.84, 6407.04) | 30 |

Counts are **samples**, not polygon-area percentages. They depend on the
grid resolution and should not be used to estimate coverage area. The
absence of a crossing from this finite grid is **not a proof of polygon
containment**. For `4104` no sampled point lies inside parent 41; this
does **not** prove the complete polygon is disjoint. Witness points have
no tested Y value, so they do not establish runtime area events or
area precedence.

The current AstaPS `WorldAreaIndex` (both `MeChen618/AstaPS` and
`RinoPaw/AstaPS`, blob `c6a15147e566b4e420c2faad105bed776c38732b`)
implements only `level1AreaAt(float x, float z)` and does not implement
`areaAt(x,y,z)` or native level-2 selection. A previous paragraph
describing a parent-gated `areaAt` as already implemented was incorrect.

## Edge-split follow-up: 23 clearance-gated interior witnesses

The 17×17 grid is finite and can miss small portions of an area. A second
algorithm splits each child polygon edge at intersections with its declared
parent polygon, tests a set of offsets **toward the child interior**, and
requires the candidate to be inside the child, outside the parent, and
**more than 0.25 world-coordinate units from both polygon boundaries**.
This filters line-coincidence and tiny rounding discrepancies.

On the same pinned scene-3 resource, it finds **23 child IDs**:
all 20 grid cases plus **202, 3606, 5502**. Extra examples:

| Child | Declared parent | Interior witness X/Z | Other matching level-1 ID | Minimum boundary clearance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 202 | 2 | (1955.6178, -1589.1993) | 1 | 1.0 |
| 3606 | 36 | (4140.3532, 4888.2848) | 35 | 2.8858 |
| 5502 | 55 | (8591.3423, 7877.7887) | 56 | 0.5 |

The machine-readable output holds all 23 exact witness coordinates.
Two independent positive-witness methods agree on the first 20. This
does **not** mean that the remaining polygons have been mathematically
proven contained: the edge algorithm also uses a finite set of inward
offsets, assumes the winding is a useful local interior direction, and
does not implement full polygon Boolean intersection. Both methods
ignore Y for identifying the conflicting X/Z outlines.

## Exact-client area request producer probe (2026-10-14)

The pinned 7.1 Global executable and metadata were successfully fetched,
hash-validated and queried in [Actions run 38487190697](https://github.com/RinoPaw/Genshin-Reverse/actions/runs/38487190697).
The request type `GEAMLDNCCEP` (CmdId 25407 / typeDefinitionIndex 82921)
has eight native methods, including `.ctor@0x104B5670`,
`CopyFrom@0x104B5690`, and a wire-writing method
`IENGFLPCLNM@0x104B5700` (its disassembly explicitly emits tags 8
and 32 for protobuf fields 1 and 4). Its metadata contains exactly two `uint32`
fields: `BDACPMHCJGH` (index 412084) and `LGANHLIBCEI`
(index 412085). The response `ICMCPHPFIKJ` (CmdId 6774) contains
the same two field-name tokens as `uint32` plus an `int32`
`BLPJNJBFDJJ`. We have **not** established which token means
`areaId` versus `areaType` from a native producer/consumer chain.

A whole-image E8 rel32 scan found **zero validated direct callers**
for the eight request methods. A complete signature scan found **zero
external methods accepting or returning that request type**. These are
negative results for the stated techniques, **not** evidence that the
request is never sent: indirect calls, generic packet dispatch and
inlining remain possible.

A second exact-sample slot-reference scan (partially successful
[run 38487515704](https://github.com/RinoPaw/Genshin-Reverse/actions/runs/38487515704))
identified a more useful candidate:

- Request class slot `0x57F2910` referenced at `0xF05DA27` by
  `KLLNGCPBLMM.FBJEEOLKAAD@0xF05DA10` (method index 615926,
  parameter type `EOFNANCNGFK`);
- the same type `KLLNGCPBLMM` has a method
  `AOBLMEIIJCC@0xF054180` whose decoded parameter is the exact
  **response** type `ICMCPHPFIKJ`;
- the other two references to the request class slot are the
  protocol registry constructor and the request type's self-registration
  method. The eight request method pointers occupy slots
  `0x2DACFD0..0x2DAD008`, with no direct RIP references identified.

Follow-up [exact-client native callgraph run 38487736933](https://github.com/RinoPaw/Genshin-Reverse/actions/runs/38487736933)
confirms that this candidate actually constructs the request. At
`0xF05DA27` it loads the request-type slot, and at `0xF05DA2E`
calls a helper accepting `Il2CppRuntimeClassHandle`. It copies the
32-bit input at `[rdi+0x5C]` into request `[rsi+0x18]`; if the input is
**1**, it derives request `[rsi+0x1C]` from input `[rdi+0x58]`;
if **2**, from input `[rdi+0x48]`. Both ID paths call the same
`0xBB15F50` conversion helper. It then passes the request object
as the second argument to `EDKMMIPJHJA.DMLCLHCBOBJ@0x7246860`.
These facts derive from the actual pinned executable, not matching
community geometry.

The `IENGFLPCLNM@0x104B5700` request writer emits wire tag
`0x08` for object offset `0x18` (protobuf field 1, varint) and
`0x20` for `0x1C` (field 4, varint). Consequently the branch
value **1/2** and the selected area ID occupy **different wire fields**.
The class field-names `BDACPMHCJGH` and `LGANHLIBCEI` are still
obfuscated. Pending native consumers, the branch value is a strong
`areaType` candidate and the converted value an `areaId` candidate.

Two independently validated direct callsites `0x8CB580F` and
`0x8CB59E0` share one caller:
`HJJINELFFMJ.MDBCMKFJJNN@0x8CB5580` (parameters `bool,bool`).
This is now the primary source of the region selection investigation.
Tracing this higher-level caller must determine where the input
`EOFNANCNGFK` stores its level-1/level-2 area IDs and what makes the
selector choose branch 1 versus 2. It is not yet established that the
caller implements level precedence exactly as the static resources imply.

This gives a candidate **client owner type** to inspect, but the
`0xF05DA10` reference could still be registration/dispatch plumbing.
Do not label it the request sender without tracing its method body or
callers. The existing generic IL2CPP field-offset resolver also found
zero valid registration candidates on these two packet types; preserve
that failure and do not fabricate object offsets. The full evidence
snapshot is in [native producer evidence](7.1-world-area-native-producer-evidence.json).

## Confirmed parent-gated child polygon search in the native 7.1 client

[Exact native probe run 38489544385](https://github.com/RinoPaw/Genshin-Reverse/actions/runs/38489544385)
disassembled `CEBMPFCJPGE.INENBAGNECA@0xD1F6C70`, called at
`0x8CB587F` by the area update method. **This establishes parent
containment as a necessary condition to traverse that parent's child
polygon candidates in this client method.**

It enumerates the parent records from `[self+0x10]`, reads each parent's
spatial shape at `[parent+0x18]`, and evaluates the position using the
shared tester at **VA** `0x151F72810` (**RVA** `0x11F72810`, using
image base `0x140000000`) with flag 0. After
`test al,al@0xD1F6D18`, **only the success branch**
`jne 0xD1F6D6D` enters the parent record's child collection
`[parent+0x10]`. The failure branch advances to the next parent
(`inc rdi@0xD1F6D1C`). The child branch loads each child shape
(`[child+0x10]`) and tests it with the same function using flag 1 at
`0xD1F6DE7`.

This is independently corroborated by the area update's two-key lookup:
`0x8CB58A3` reads a key from its chosen first-stage area record,
`0x8CB58B2` normalizes that key, and `0x8CB58D1` performs a nested
dictionary lookup using it and the child spatial candidate.
`0x1366D2F0` resolves the nested dictionary and can return null
when the child is missing. The code-path conclusion is stronger than
the earlier hypothesis, though the exact semantic name of the
first-stage record's `+0x4C` member is not established.

**Implication for the 24 cross-scene positive interior crossing witnesses:** given
the recorded community geometry, a child polygon's portion outside its
declared parent cannot be reached through the matching parent's
child-traversal branch at those X/Z coordinates. This is a model
prediction using historical community geometries, **not** a native 7.1
runtime observation of those coordinates; independent extraction of
the actual client area assets is still needed.

The parent/child tester flags, inclusive Y comparisons and
first-hit order have now been confirmed in the native spatial method,
as documented below. Remaining unknowns are the actual 7.1 asset
geometry, missing-range defaults and higher-level event timing.

## Confirmed native height flag and first-match selection order

[Exact 7.1 sample probe 38490261052](https://github.com/RinoPaw/Genshin-Reverse/actions/runs/38490261052)
resolved the common spatial predicate: binary **RVA `0x11F72810`**
(loaded **VA `0x151F72810`**, with image base `0x140000000`).
It reads the tested position as `Vector3`: X at `+0x0`, Y at
`+0x4`, and Z at `+0x8`. Bounding-box minima are at
shape offsets `0x24/0x28/0x2C` and maxima `0x30/0x34/0x38`.

It **always checks X and Z bounds**; then at
`0x11F7285E` tests the boolean flag. For flag 0 it skips the Y
range; for flag 1 it rejects `y < minY` and `y > maxY`
at `0x11F72863–0x11F72876`. The remaining method obtains polygon
data at `shape+0x10` and performs a point-in-X/Z-polygon test.
Therefore the native parent scan uses **X/Z only** (flag 0), while the
child scan uses **X/Z plus inclusive Y limits** (flag 1).
This establishes the boolean semantics at the actual callsites, not
just a guessed interpretation of resource `minY/maxY`.

The child scan `CEBMPFCJPGE.INENBAGNECA@0xD1F6C70` has a further
observable precedence rule. It scans **parent polygons in list order**,
and only the **first parent hit** enters that parent's child list.
Children are then scanned in their list order and the first spatial
match is returned. If the first matched parent contains no qualifying
child, `0xD1F6DFE` jumps to the null return at `0xD1F6E43`:
it **does not retry other overlapping parents**. This is confirmed
inside that native method, but is not yet a complete specification of
higher-level client state changes, sent events or polygon provenance.

The exact native geometries have still not been independently extracted
for 7.1. A missing height maximum in a community record cannot be
assumed to mean a specific numeric default until the native asset
loader is examined.

## Native-confirmed original WorldArea root array count codec

The first **five bytes** of all 13 hash-verified native 7.1
WorldArea payloads match the root-array reader independently
recovered from the exact 7.1 client.

At `CEBMPFCJPGE.GMENPOPMKAA@0xD1F6FA0`, the parser
consumes one byte and, for its signed-bit branch, dispatches to
array reader **RVA `0x82093B0`**. Every original scene payload
starts with the marker `0xCB`, which takes that branch. The
array reader consumes the following 4-byte unsigned little-endian
word and **adds `0x91B65B45` modulo 2³²**:
`mov edx, 0x91B65B45@0x82093FD`,
`add edx, [rax+rcx+0x20]@0x8209402`.
That is exactly equivalent to subtracting `0x6E49A4BB`,
the constant previously derived from independent sample comparisons.

As a result, the 13 exact-client top-level decoded array lengths
are **65, 4, 1, 1, 1, 4, 4, 1, 3, 1, 3, 1, 1**
in scene ID order
`3,4,5,6,7,9,10,11,100,101,102,103,51195`.
All 13 match their historical community top-level WorldArea
parent-record counts (**90 total**). The reader allocates an array
with that recovered count and loops over elements, passing each
element to `0x82092C0`.

This is now **native code-confirmed root count decoding**, not a
constant fitted solely to JSON. The field-level structure of each
element, child IDs, polygon vertices and height ranges are **not
yet decoded or compared**. In particular 13-of-13 root count
agreement does not resolve the 14 historically missing child
geometries. Full evidence: [native header decode](7.1-native-world-area-header-correlation.json)

## Native per-area record and child-array reader

[Native run 38493114804](https://github.com/RinoPaw/Genshin-Reverse/actions/runs/38493114804)
continued from the confirmed root count reader `0x82093B0`.
Each root element is parsed by `0x82092C0`, which reads a
single-byte field mask transformed with `(byte+0x15) XOR 0x55`
and dispatches by bits `0x40` and `0x01` to two nested
field readers. All thirteen original payloads' **first** root
field masks agree with the corresponding historical first
parent's child-list presence: 9 have bit `0x01` present, 4
have it absent. This is only a first-item presence check,
not a whole-scene comparison.

Exact native decoding then identifies `0x87B02F0` as an
**array of area records**. It reads `uint32_le XOR
0xFBD01BCE` as its count and calls `0x87B0460` once per
item. The latter parses a spatial area record with an
obfuscated presence byte (`byte+0xFC`, modulo 256),
a nested shape object at record offset `+0x10`, and
32-bit scalar members at `+0x18` and `+0x1C`.
Its corresponding scalar transforms include
`raw+0x7B4A79A4` and `raw XOR 0xB3B8156D`
depending on the field mask. The nested spatial
polygon/height reader is called at **RVA `0x11F72A40`**.

These are native layout/control-flow facts, still short of
a complete reader. The IDs/meaning of `+0x18` and `+0x1C`
must be proven by exact decoded records, and the nested
shape/vertex stream needs full byte-consumption checks.
See [schema leads](7.1-native-world-area-schema-leads.json).

## Engineering implications

1. Keep `areaID2=0` for the thirteen unmapped areas rather than assign
   ungrounded polygons. `0` means unresolved, **not** the actual game's
   area ID.
2. Do not silently change those four child area parent IDs or force a
   foreign parent's child into a parent's area based on centroid alone.
3. AstaPS currently implements only a level-1 polygon lookup.
   The exact 7.1 client **does** parent-gate child polygon traversal.
   If a level-2 server lookup is implemented for parity, preserve that
   dependency, test all 24 cross-scene interior crossing witnesses and do not claim
   full parity without native 7.1 geometry and height-rule verification.
4. Do not use any of these ambiguous IDs for irreversible chest
   reward classification until their selection is verified.
5. The exact 7.1 executable and all thirteen original WorldArea
   binary payloads are now accessible in reproducible research
   workflows. Recover their native deserializer, compare every polygon
   and missing child ID against the community data, then corroborate
   important discrepancies with runtime area events. Record exact
   sample hash, positions, height, area IDs and area types.
