**13,007/14,084 (92.35%)** have an identifiable level-1 area/city through
their authored scene+coordinates. The remaining 1,077 live in scenes
without WorldArea geometry (many dungeon/quest scenes). These must **not**
inherit an arbitrary open-world country reward.

A `parent_only` result is not a failed city lookup; it means area2 is
unresolved or absent at that height. It is not safe to force a neighboring
area2 ID. The 136 `changed_native_geometry` matches use **historical**
community polygons with known discrepancies from the pinned original 7.1
asset; they are **not** a count of all truly affected chest coordinates.

## Blocking questions for *reward* partition

1. In old Mondstadt/Liyue, standard common/exquisite/precious Primogem
   payouts are generally 0/2/5; named high-rate areas are Dragonspine,
   Stormbearer Mountains, Guyun Stone Forest, Lisha and The Chasm
   (see [Chest](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Chest)).
   **These exceptions still need a verified `scene/area1/area2` mapping.**
   Strong Snow Mountain tag evidence exists for `scene3:area1=10`.
2. The pinned `TextMapCHS.json` is a flat numeric-hash dictionary, but
   **none of the 578 WorldArea `areaNameTextMapHash` values resolve** in
   that snapshot (documented by the diagnostic run). Do not manufacture
   Chinese area labels. Find a matching client localization or alternate
   hash/name mapping before applying area-based low/high reward overrides.
3. Separate quest/special prefabs from ordinary treasure boxes. The scan
   intentionally includes explicit-ID puzzle/quest objects, and the
   3,725 no-metadata objects are not proof of ordinary loot.
4. Compare the coordinate positions to **exact 7.1 geometry** where child
   IDs 5502, 5602, 5608, 5701, 5702, 5703, 5802 and 5804 or adjacent
   precedence could matter. The original 7.1 geometry was already decoded
   in [7.1 WorldArea study](7.1-world-area-boundary-audit.md);
   do not repeat that binary research.
5. Audit Shrine/Obelisk/Remarkable/event rewards separately; an area
   selector does not identify an ordinary chest or prevent a double grant.

## Decision

**Safe to implement now:** deterministic `scene+position → area1 → cityId`
lookup; optional `area2` using the validated parent/height rules;
whole-table selector priority `kind+area2 > kind+area1 > kind+city > kind`;
explicit unknown handling.

**Not safe to activate yet:** national `common-low` payout overrides
without named high-zone exceptions; treating `drop_tag`'s 'low grade'
as a low-Primogem region; paying all `Chest` gadgets a fixed amount.

Further work should map the named exceptions using an independently
verified localization and restrict the reward population to genuine
ordinary chests before a server payout change.
