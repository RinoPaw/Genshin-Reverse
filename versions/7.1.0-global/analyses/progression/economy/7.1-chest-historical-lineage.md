## Native 7.1 promotion gates

1. **Object identity:** recover Gadget config asset and schema/load path from the pinned **Global 7.1** client. Cross-check against the already-maintained independent CN 7.1 GadgetExcel comparison in [the chest reward study](7.1-chest-reward-evidence.md); do not mistake agreement of 309 `Chest` rows for verified native original bytes.
2. **Instance authenticity:** find an exact source for each scene/group/config. Determine whether the client even ships `Scripts/Scene`/`drop_tag`; these may be server-only and unavailable from the client executable. Distinguish missing evidence from absence in the game.
3. **Reward ownership:** verify whether any client- or server-authored `chest_drop_id` references a nonempty payable root. Special/quest/remarkable chests, sentinel roots and tagless instances remain separately unresolved until proven.
4. **Probability:** require authenticated 7.1 server source/consumer or appropriately controlled official-server observations before accepting exact default item weights. Historical config/derived classes/custom rules must retain their own status.

The current AstaPS Loot branch uses `ChestTypeResolver.fromExploreKind("chest_puzzle") -> normal`, grade heuristics in `WorldChestLootHelper.resolveTier()`, and geography/default distributions in `chests.css`. These are implementation policy. A tag derived from an imported marker is **not** a recovered original scene field. Keep Loot parsing and claim validation separate from reward authenticity.

Comparing unique `(最小等级, 总索引)` keys, then `掉落ID` and `掉落次数`, yields **548** early rows, **980** later rows, **269** shared keys, **213** matching root/count pairs, **56** changed root/count pairs, **279** earlier-only keys and **711** later-only keys. The level-1 Mondstadt combat/puzzle roots `51010000`/`52010000` reach **15/17** table nodes; at level 26, `51010500`/`52010500` reach **26/27**. Expanded historical graph signatures differ. Their common direct children include item IDs `102`, `305` and `202` with counts `10`, `1` and `240;360` at level 1; no payout amounts or weights have been verified as native 7.1.

[YuukiPS/GIO-Resources 3.2](https://gitlab.com/YuukiPS/GIO-Resources/-/tree/3.2/txt) lists the four filenames in a directory with a **2023-01-03** commit (`3952ccb8`).
